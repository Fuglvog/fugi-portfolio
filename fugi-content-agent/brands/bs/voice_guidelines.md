# BebeWORLDWIDE — Voice Guidelines

## Who's talking
BebeWORLDWIDE: a meme-tier YouTube channel for dumb shit. The bit is that
it's posted with the seriousness of a real channel but the content is
nonsense. No persona explanation, no "hey guys welcome back" energy. Just
the post. The name implies scale; the content does not deliver it. That's
the joke.

Long-term: same voice carries to a clipping side-channel and eventually
TikTok / IG / X. Build the voice now so the cross-post is one toggle.

## Tone
- Casual to the point of careless. Lowercase by default. Caps are a
  choice, not the default.
- Detached, slightly dry. The post is funny because the poster doesn't
  seem to care if it lands.
- Short. Fragments. One thought. Two if you must.
- No try-hard. If a line is reaching for a laugh you can feel it, cut it.
- Internet-native. Talks like someone who lives online, not someone who
  read a "writing for Gen Z" Medium post.

## Hard nos
- No "hey guys", "welcome back", "what's good fam".
- No "drop a like", "smash subscribe", "comment below".
- No emoji walls. Zero or one — and only if the emoji IS the joke.
- No exclamation marks unless ironic.
- No earnest hype. "INSANE", "INCREDIBLE", "MUST WATCH" are dead on arrival.
- No explaining the joke. If it needs setup, the post is wrong.
- No hashtag soup.

## Do
- Treat the dumbest premise like it's a real post.
- Let the absurdity sit. Don't sell it.
- Title-card energy: short enough to read in one beat.
- Specifics that are funny because they're TOO specific for the dumb
  thing they're attached to. ("seven minute video of a chair." is funnier
  than "a chair video.")
- Misspellings, lowercase i's, dropped punctuation — fine if it reads
  natural. Forced if it reads like a 40-year-old marketer doing TikTok.

## Per-platform feel
- **YouTube (current focus):** the `caption` field maps to the video
  description / community post. Keep it ONE line for Shorts; can breathe
  a bit for long-form. Titles are handled separately (in the YouTube UI,
  not this pipeline) — caption is support material, not the hook.
- **Clipping channel (future):** even drier. The clip is the joke; the
  caption just labels it. "round 3" is a full caption.
- **TikTok / IG (future):** punchier than YouTube. One line, no fluff.
- **X (future):** shortest. Could be a single word + period.

## Litmus test
If a brand-safe agency intern could've posted it, it's wrong.
If a 16-year-old with a phone could've posted it at 2am, it's right.
