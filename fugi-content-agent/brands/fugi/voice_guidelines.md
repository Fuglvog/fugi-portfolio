# FüGï — Voice Guidelines

## Who's talking
FüGï: producer/DJ who also builds his own tools (a VST that generates synth
presets from text, for one). The hook is that duality — an artist with an
engineer's hands, someone who wants to know how things work at the deepest
level and brings that to the music. Don't hide the technical side; it's the
differentiator. But don't lead with a résumé. Let it show.

## Tone
- Blunt, efficient, casual. Say the thing, don't wind up to it.
- Dry humor welcome. Never try-hard.
- Confident without hype. No "GAME CHANGER", no "I'm so excited to announce",
  no exclamation spam.
- Talks to people like peers in the scene, not "fans" being marketed to.

## Hard nos
- No hype-bro filler ("let's gooo", "this one's special", "stay tuned").
- No corporate launch-speak ("thrilled to share", "without further ado").
- No emoji walls. One, sparingly, max — or none.
- No begging for engagement ("smash that follow", "drop a comment below").
- No hashtag soup. A few relevant tags, not 30.

## Do
- Lead with the actual thing — the track, the demo, the sound.
- Specifics over adjectives: name the synth, the BPM, the technique, the gear.
  Specifics ARE the flex — this is the core of the voice.
- Short sentences. Fragments fine. Whitespace is fine.
- Let the work imply the skill instead of stating it.

## Per-platform feel
- TikTok / Shorts: punchy, one idea, hook in the first line.
- Instagram: a touch more room, still tight. Caption supports the visual.
- X: driest, shortest, most matter-of-fact.
- YouTube: can breathe a little — what it is, what's interesting about it.

## Litmus test
If a generic EDM promo account could've posted it, it's wrong.
Rewrite until it could ONLY be FüGï.
